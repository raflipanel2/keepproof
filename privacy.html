Privacy policy

How KeepProof handles local tools, advertising scripts and website privacy.

Our website and local tools

KeepProof is a static educational website. The Purchase Register saves typed entries to localStorage in the browser on the device you use, not to a KeepProof account. The Support Packet Builder processes your text locally and does not upload it; the Return Window Calendar performs date arithmetic in your browser.

Control and removal

You can delete individual purchase entries in the register, use Clear Records to remove entries, or clear this site’s browser storage. Deleting site data, using a private browsing window or changing devices can remove local entries. Exported CSV and TXT files remain wherever you save them.

Advertising and external services

Our homepage and educational articles contain a Google AdSense script for site verification and possible advertising. Google and its partners may process information, use cookies or similar technologies, and serve personalized or non-personalized advertising according to their own policies and applicable laws. We do not operate or control Google’s ad systems. Google privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy . External source links also follow their own privacy practices.

No request for sensitive data

Our tools are designed for product names, ordinary dates and file-location notes. Do not enter passwords, payment-card numbers, identity-document details or sensitive information. We do not intentionally collect these through a contact form, and we have no user accounts.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email raflipanel2@gmail.com. This policy reflects the static-site and browser-storage implementation prepared 11 November 2026. It should be reviewed again if tracking, forms, analytics, hosting or advertising practices change. A browser or hosting provider may log technical request data under its own service practices.

KEEP THE QUESTIONS CLEAR

For product-specific decisions, use the official seller policy and the actual contract. We are an independent education site.
