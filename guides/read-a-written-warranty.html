FIELD GUIDE 05 / WARRANTIES

Read a written warranty before you rely on it

The clauses that change coverage, costs and repair options.

Published: 11 November 2026 · KeepProof Editorial · General education

“Two-year warranty” sounds simple, but duration is only one part of the promise. The same product can have different coverage for the motor, battery, accessories, labor and shipping. Reading the contract as a sequence of questions is more useful than memorizing the headline number.

01Identify who makes the promise

Find the warrantor’s legal name and the country or market covered. The seller, manufacturer and optional service-plan administrator may be separate organizations. Write down the official claims channel and whether the policy directs you to the store first.

Locate the effective date: purchase, delivery, registration or another event. Do not assume it begins on the day you opened the box. If the document is a limited warranty, locate the actual exclusions instead of guessing based on the label.

02Build a coverage table

Make four columns: covered component, covered failure, excluded situation and remedy. For example, a small appliance might cover an internal manufacturing defect but not ordinary wear, misuse or damage from a drop. This is an illustration, not a statement about any particular brand.

Look for parts and labor limits. A repair may be free while shipping is paid by the owner, or the contract may require the owner to deliver the unit to a service center. Check whether replacement restarts coverage.

03Understand claim duties

The contract may tell you to retain proof of purchase, register a product, describe the defect, obtain authorization or use specified packaging. The FTC’s US guidance also explains important legal limits on warranty restrictions; requirements differ by jurisdiction.

If a support representative makes an additional promise, ask for confirmation by email. Keep dates and reference numbers. A verbal assurance is harder to show later than a written support record.

04Keep the exact version you relied on

Download the warranty PDF and save the policy-page URL with the date you read it. Online policies may be revised for future sales. Your specific documents and local law determine the actual rights and responsibilities.

If the terms are unclear, write down the question before contacting support. “Does the battery have the same coverage duration as the main device?” is more likely to get a useful answer than “Is everything covered?”

IN PRACTICE / EXAMPLE

An ordinary example

A blender is advertised with a five-year motor warranty, but the jar and accessories may have different terms. The buyer records the motor coverage, exclusions, proof requirement and shipping procedure before purchase, rather than treating five years as universal coverage.

TAKE THIS WITH YOU

Action checklist

  1. Identify the warrantor and start date.
  2. Separate components, failures and exclusions.
  3. Record who pays for shipping and labor.
  4. Save the warranty document as sold.
