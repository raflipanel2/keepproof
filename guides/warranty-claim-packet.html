FIELD GUIDE 07 / WARRANTIES

Prepare a warranty claim evidence packet

A clean, factual package that avoids repeat support requests.

Published: 11 November 2026 · KeepProof Editorial · General education

A warranty claim is easier to evaluate when a support representative can understand the product, the problem and the steps already taken without guessing. A good packet is short, organized and truthful. It avoids irrelevant personal data and does not promise that the defect will be covered.

01Create a one-page timeline

Start with product model, purchase date, purchase channel and a clear symptom. Record the first observed failure and whether it happens consistently or intermittently. If safe, note how the issue can be reproduced without dangerous repair instructions.

Example: “On November 8, the left speaker stopped playing audio after a normal restart. The issue occurs on two devices with the supplied cable.” That gives the agent a narrow problem to investigate. Avoid diagnosing unseen internal components.

02Attach only relevant evidence

Typically useful records include the invoice, a model-label image, the written warranty, photographs or video demonstrating the symptom, and correspondence with the seller. If shipping damage is involved, preserve packaging photographs and the delivery timeline.

Do not send a full bank statement if a redacted receipt satisfies the request. Never include passwords, login recovery codes or unrelated personal files in a support upload.

03Ask for an actionable next step

Contact the official seller or warrantor through the channel specified in the terms. Ask whether the issue appears eligible for assessment, how to obtain return authorization, and who covers shipping or diagnostic charges.

Record the support case number, date, representative’s name when provided, and the promised next step. If a phone conversation includes a material decision, request email confirmation.

04Keep a consistent record after shipping

Photograph the product condition before dispatch and retain tracking and packaging evidence. If service replaces a component or the entire product, ask for written details about the work and any remaining coverage.

Escalate based on the actual policy and local consumer protections, not on a generic claim that all failures legally require replacement. The FTC offers US-specific guidance for unresolved disputes; other jurisdictions have different processes.

IN PRACTICE / EXAMPLE

An ordinary example

A coffee machine begins leaking from a seam after normal cleaning. The owner submits a 90-word description, invoice PDF, model plate image, and a short video showing where water appears. The support team can request a service authorization without first asking which model was purchased.

TAKE THIS WITH YOU

Action checklist

  1. Write a factual symptom timeline.
  2. Collect receipt, model and policy together.
  3. Use only official support channels.
  4. Keep case numbers, shipping and final service notes.
