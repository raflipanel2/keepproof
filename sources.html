Sources and further reading

Primary consumer education references used by KeepProof.

US Federal Trade Commission — warranties

A consumer-oriented explanation of written and implied warranties, service contracts, recordkeeping and resolving purchase problems. https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/warranties

FTC — returns and refunds

Practical advice for learning the store policy, keeping proof of purchase and contacting the business. https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/solving-problems-business-returns-refunds-and-other-resolutions

FTC — online shopping

Practical guidance on checking sellers, retaining purchase records and handling online-shopping problems. https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/online-shopping

FTC — holiday gift returns

Explains why gift return policies and exceptions vary and why receipts matter. https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2023/12/what-know-about-holiday-gift-returns

USAGov — complaints about products and services

Guidance on documenting a complaint and seeking further consumer assistance when necessary. https://www.usa.gov/company-product-service-complaints

FTC — federal warranty law

More detailed US legal framework for written warranties. This is US-specific and should not be treated as legal advice elsewhere. https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/businesspersons-guide-federal-warranty-law

How to use sources responsibly

Check the publication date and whether a rule or contract applies to your location, product category and transaction date. The original purchase documents and official current policy should take priority over a general summary.
