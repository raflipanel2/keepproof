About KeepProof

What KeepProof covers, who it is for, and what the publication does not claim.

A small independent reference desk

KeepProof is an independent educational publishing project about keeping purchase evidence and making sense of written return and warranty terms. It is designed for people who want clear organizational habits rather than product rankings, aggressive sales pages or promises of a legal outcome.

Our scope

We publish practical guides, worked hypothetical examples and small browser-based worksheets. The guides explain common document types, how to verify a seller’s written policies and which questions to ask. No item on the website is a personalized legal opinion, an insurance policy interpretation, or a guarantee of refund, repair or coverage.

Editorial responsibility

Content is prepared and maintained under the KeepProof Editorial byline. We use named primary references and publish limitations rather than invent credentials or pretend that staff members tested products they have not tested. Our detailed methodology is available on the editorial standards page.

Contact the publisher

Corrections, feedback and editorial inquiries can be sent to raflipanel2@gmail.com. This address is the publication’s contact point. We are not a marketplace, store, courier, warranty administrator or customer-service agent for any brand.

KEEP THE QUESTIONS CLEAR

For product-specific decisions, use the official seller policy and the actual contract. We are an independent education site.
